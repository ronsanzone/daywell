# Daywell 0.1.0 — Development Preview 2

## What's new

- **Inline reminder lists:** My Day and its pin stay at the top, with list counts
  and short previews below. Secondary sections and lists start collapsed.
- Press **s** to expand/collapse all sections; click a heading to toggle one.
  Use **+** to choose a reminder for My Day without moving it or changing its deadline.
- Wider, borderless native menu-bar panel with persistent capture and improved
  positioning, dismissal, and focus behavior.
- Safer capture: submitted options survive asynchronous work; a refresh failure
  cannot discard a successfully saved reminder's requested My Day membership.
  Partial success is reported without encouraging duplicate creation.
- Fresh capture respects the preferred inbox or Apple Reminders default, while
  retained drafts keep their chosen list.
- Confirmed day-plan backup recovery preserves corrupt originals and refuses
  to overwrite newer unsupported schemas.
- Consistent busy/read-only action checks, isolated regression fixtures, pinned
  formatting/lint checks, dependency boundaries, and macOS CI.

## Download and run

- Download **Daywell-v0.1.0-dev.2-macos-arm64.zip** and unzip it to obtain `Daywell.app`.
- Requires an **Apple Silicon Mac, macOS 14 or later**. This is not an Intel build.
- The app is **ad-hoc signed, not Developer ID signed or notarized**. macOS may
  block downloaded copies. Review the app and use the per-app **Open Anyway**
  control in System Settings → Privacy & Security if offered. Do not disable
  Gatekeeper globally.
- Quit an older Daywell instance before launching this build. A rebuilt or
  transferred copy may require fresh Reminders permission.
- To try disposable demo data without accessing Apple Reminders:
  `open /path/to/Daywell.app --args --demo`.
- In normal mode, completing, editing, capturing, or deleting reminders changes
  real Apple Reminders data. Grant access only when ready to use that behavior.

## Verification and limitations

- All **61 automated tests** passed, plus formatting, lint, dependency boundaries,
  and debug/release builds.
- **39 native UI checks** passed in both debug and the packaged release executable,
  including pointer list expansion and selection, section shortcuts, capture,
  pinning, completion/undo, and panel dismissal.
- Fresh screenshots reviewed across all three themes and empty/error/permission
  states; the packaged build's main panel was also rendered and inspected.
- Bundle signature and metadata validated; the ZIP was extracted and its executable
  compared with the tested binary.
- Live EventKit acceptance was **not rerun for this release**. Previous live results
  apply to the previous build. Full VoiceOver/Tab traversal, macOS 14 compatibility,
  large stores, and broader integration coverage remain incomplete.
- When Apple Reminders advances a repeating series, Daywell still disables
  completion undo rather than moving the next occurrence backward. Ordinary
  completion retains undo.

This is a development preview, not a production release. App version: **0.1.0 (2)**.
