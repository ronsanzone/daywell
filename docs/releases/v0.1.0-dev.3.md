# Daywell 0.1.0 — Development Preview 3

## What's new

- **Amber CRT:** opt-in amber typography and phosphor glow, with Metal-generated
  scanlines, grain, and vignette. Adjust CRT intensity in Settings; try **85%**.
- **Orbital Relay:** a small decorative instrument beside My Day that aligns on
  opening and rests between slow rotations. It is not a sync or task-status indicator.
- **Phosphor selection trails:** focus changes immediately; the previous outline
  fades briefly without smearing task text.
- **Completion and Undo traces:** a checked chip contracts toward Done today after
  a confirmed save; Undo returns it. Failed or unconfirmed writes do not celebrate.
- **Task patchbay:** press **b** or use the header button for side-by-side source
  lists and My Day. Choose with **+**, **a**, or drag across; chosen reminders stay
  in their original Apple lists. Amber sends a short signal through the gutter.
- **Motion controls:** Settings → Terminal motion disables animations independently
  of CRT intensity. Motion also respects macOS Reduce Motion and stops when the
  panel closes. Other themes remain still; the patchbay works in every theme.
- A confirmed completion remains visible even if the following refresh fails.

## Download and run

- Download **Daywell-v0.1.0-dev.3-macos-arm64.zip** and unzip it to obtain `Daywell.app`.
- Requires an **Apple Silicon Mac, macOS 14 or later**. This is not an Intel build.
  No compiler or Xcode is needed to run the downloaded app.
- The app is **ad-hoc signed, not Developer ID signed or notarized**. macOS may
  block downloaded copies. After reviewing the app, use the per-app **Open Anyway**
  control in System Settings → Privacy & Security if offered. Do not disable
  Gatekeeper globally.
- Quit an older Daywell instance before launching this build. Rebuilding or
  transferring the app may require fresh Reminders permission.
- Graphite is still the default. Select **Amber CRT · experimental** in Settings,
  then set intensity to **85%** to try the new look with your normal configuration.
- To try disposable demo data without accessing Apple Reminders:

  ```sh
  open /path/to/Daywell.app --args --demo --theme amber --crt-intensity 0.85
  ```

- Demo preferences are temporary. In normal mode, completion, editing, capture,
  and deletion change real Apple Reminders data; choosing for My Day changes only
  the local plan.

## Verification and limitations

- **71 automated tests** passed, along with formatting, lint, dependency boundaries,
  and debug/release builds.
- The **packaged release executable passed 58 native UI checks in each of Graphite
  and Amber**, including patchbay pointer/keyboard actions, completion/Undo,
  the motion toggle, and panel dismissal/reopening.
- Inspected a fresh packaged-build patchbay render at 85% CRT. Implementation
  verification also inspected animation keyframes and empty/error/permission states,
  and confirmed pixel-stable transitions with motion disabled.
- Bundle signature and metadata validated; the ZIP was extracted and its executable
  and metadata compared with the tested bundle.
- Physical drag/drop, full VoiceOver/Tab traversal, switching macOS Reduce Motion
  itself, macOS 14 hardware, and large reminder stores still need manual acceptance.
- Live EventKit acceptance was **not rerun for this release**. When Apple Reminders
  advances a repeating series, completion undo remains unavailable rather than
  moving the next occurrence backward. Ordinary completion retains undo.

This is a development preview, not a production release. App version: **0.1.0 (3)**.
